# Viewer guidelines

## UI design principles

1. **Why is the user here?** Start with their purpose and the decision they
   need to make. That determines what deserves attention.

2. **A good visual needs little explanation.** Treat explanatory text as a
   design cost. If understanding requires paragraphs, improve the visual's
   structure, labels, or comparison.

3. **The first view should reveal where to go deeper.** Show enough visual
   information to recognize patterns, judge their importance, and choose what
   to investigate. Progressive disclosure starts with an informative overview.
