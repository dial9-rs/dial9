Requires positive sampling support and complete trace delivery. An estimated population by itself is insufficient.

The sampler emits startup weight 1/(f·r), using a fresh admission draw independent of the census hash. A sealed fresh thinning round uses q and multiplies each survivor’s existing weight by 1/q once. This preserves weighted sample mass in expectation even when the cutoff depends on the census. It does not use R/f divided by the realized profiler count. Missing spans, zero-admission cohorts, or missing trace delivery still prevent reconstruction of the complete fleet.

Simulated weight changes and the segment snapshot

These examples show simulated weight changes. Production metadata carries one dial9.sampling.weight snapshot captured when the recorder evaluates its source, plus the pool key. Existing boot_id metadata and normal trace clocks supply identity and time. A reader can apply the scalar to its segment; this approximates changes within the segment. Zero means stopped when evaluated, and null or missing means unknown. The simulation evaluates weights at the selected instant; it does not measure the extra error from coarse segment snapshots.

How a process reaches its decision

1 / identityOne stable tracking hash

Hash scope, dimensions, and the rank identity. Fresh default identities distinguish process runs. Simultaneously live ranks must be independent.

2 / participationOnly the lowest f slice does I/O

f = 1 by default. A lower population bound may reduce f. Tracking uses this hash. Admission uses a separate independent random draw.

3 / application startupOne roster GET, then a frozen choice

The default budget is 1,000 ms. A settled roster supplies admission probability r within the tracked slice. Selected traces begin with weight 1/(f·r).

4 / background censusCount, publish, optionally thin

One fenced leader lists two buckets. Four plateau counts adjust future admissions. Over the soft trigger, a fresh round q = target / admitted count independently stops profilers. New startups skip earlier rounds.

Off stays off; stopped stays stopped

Unarmed processes and trimmed carriers still count toward the tracked population. They cannot turn on later. A shrinking fleet can therefore fall below the target until future startups arrive. Automatic recorder registration connects terminal stop decisions to the recorder.

The profiler count is probabilistic

Target + ceil(3√target) triggers thinning. A simultaneous round retains Binomial(A, q) profilers, centered on the target; it can still exceed the trigger. Polls apply rounds asynchronously. Existing profilers keep running during storage failures, so an outage has no unconditional count cap.

Simulation events and accuracy limits

Event model: independent uniform tracking ranks and separate admission draws; immediate successful storage operations and complete trace delivery; one abstract elected leader with a three-poll lease; 60-second polls with ±10% fixed jitter; per-process heartbeat offsets within 300-second buckets; 11-second bucket-edge guard; four ≤2% growth readings; sealed fresh thinning rounds observed once at polls, with a 190-second cooldown while still above the trigger. A fresh census at or below the trigger clears the round. New joiners skip rounds already present at their startup. Optional crash/cleanup ghosts and storage outages model bias. Rolling fleets begin warm and overlap old/new processes by 15 seconds. This is not an S3 throughput, latency, or full concurrent-CAS benchmark. Partial LIST failures, delayed network responses, and clock-bound violations require separate adversarial checks.
